# Glossary for Zelda: Oracle of Ages (Greek)

Master renderings are polytonic. They are kept consistent with the ALttP translation wherever a term is shared.

**Tags:** V = taken from your ALttP translation (seen in batch 1 and your screenshots). P = my proposal for a term new to Ages. C = shared with ALttP, but I have not seen your rendering: replace the entry with yours from `greek_master.json` before use.

## Rules

- Foreign names are transliterated NT-style and are indeclinable unless a note gives endings.
- Named things take a capital on each significant word, as in ALttP (τὸ Κύριον Ξίφος, ὁ Δρυμὸς τῆς Πλάνης).
- `[Name]` and every other bracket code is copied unchanged and never inflected.
- One English word, one Greek word: sword is always ξίφος; princess βασιλίς, queen βασίλισσα; wizard/sorceress μάγος; dungeon (prison and dungeon building) δεσμωτήριον; Cane βακτηρία, Rod ῥάβδος.
- Prefer NT and LXX vocabulary. A note marks anything classical.
